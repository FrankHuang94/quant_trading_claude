    Learning paths¶

    TL;DR

    • Start with strategies & algorithms. Modules 02 and 03 carry this KB. Read market structure only as far as a strategy page needs it; firms, infrastructure and regulation come later.
    • Three paths: a 90-minute sprint (7 pages, skimmed), the 4-hour Core Path (14 pages, ~24.7k words, ~85% from modules 02–03) and a 2-week Deep Path (all of 02 and 03 with labs and walkthroughs, then the support modules, quizzes and flashcards).
    • Times assume ~100–110 words a minute. That is under half the 238 words a minute adults average on non-fiction [Verified], because these pages are dense with tables, formulas and diagrams [Estimate].
    • Labs are optional on the sprint, recommended on the Core Path and part of the Deep Path. Each runs in about a minute on a laptop.

    Level: Core · Read time: ~5 min · Last verified: 2026-11

    The 90-minute sprint¶

    Goal: one business from each end of the horizon axis and enough vocabulary to follow a quant. On each dossier read the TL;DR, header table, worked example and diagram; skip the collapsed math.

    • The big picture: firm types, horizons and who pays (10 min)
    • Strategies: the map: both maps and the "who pays you" table (10 min)
    • There is no single algorithm: "The short answer" and "The five pipelines side by side" (15 min)
    • Equity market making: the market-maker dossier, including its P&L decomposition (15 min)
    • Stat arb: overview and history: the stat-arb dossier (15 min)
    • Walkthrough: a mid-frequency stat-arb book: skim stages 1–9 and the attribution table (12 min)
    • One-page cheat sheet: formulas, numbers and terms (8 min)

    That is 85 minutes, with five to spare.

    The 4-hour Core Path¶

    These are the 14 pages listed in scripts/core_path.txt, in order. They total ~24.7k prose words, ~85% of them from modules 02–03, and carry no TODO(verify) markers. The order runs from the maps and the evaluation lens to market making (microseconds), then stat arb and cross-sectional alpha (days), then the portfolio and execution stages that turn a forecast into P&L. Labs are synthetic-data scripts: run one with python labs/NN-name/lab.py and read its README's "What this toy misses vs the real world".

    • 1. The big picture (12 min)
    • 2. Life of a stock order (10 min). Optional lab: 18 smart order router
    • 3. The limit order book (12 min). Lab: 01 matching engine
    • 4. Strategies: the map (14 min)
    • 5. How quants evaluate a strategy (22 min). Lab: 13 backtest overfitting
    • 6. There is no single algorithm (28 min)
    • 7. Equity market making (20 min). Labs: 02 microprice, 04 markouts
    • 8. Quoting and inventory control (16 min). Lab: 05 Avellaneda-Stoikov and GLFT
    • 9. Stat arb: overview and history (20 min). Lab: 09 PCA residual stat arb
    • 10. Cross-sectional alpha and neutralization (18 min). Lab: 11 signal transforms and IC
    • 11. Evaluating signals (12 min). Lab: re-read Lab 11's IC and decay output
    • 12. Mean-variance optimization (16 min). Lab: 14 risk model and MVO
    • 13. Implementation shortfall and Almgren-Chriss (12 min). Lab: 16 Almgren-Chriss frontier
    • 14. Walkthrough: a mid-frequency stat-arb book (24 min). Script: mid-frequency-stat-arb.py, which reuses labs 11, 12, 14 and 17

    Reading time is 236 minutes; each lab adds 5–10. Finish with the strategies quiz and the algorithms quiz.

    The 2-week Deep Path¶

    Fourteen sessions of 2–2.5 hours, ~30 hours in all. Days 1–12 cover every page of modules 02 and 03, grouped by business so each strategy sits next to its algorithms; days 13–14 add the context modules. Budget ~10 minutes per lab: run it, read the plot, read the "toy misses" list.

    • Day 1 · Orientation (~1 h 45 min). Big picture, cheat sheet, strategy map, how quants evaluate, algorithms overview, no single algorithm, algorithm map. New to microstructure? Add life of an order and the order book (+25 min).
    • Day 2 · Liquidity provision (~1 h 50 min). The five A dossiers and M1, the HFT loop. Lab 01.
    • Day 3 · Market-making algorithms (~2 h 25 min). M2 fair value through M9 risk controls. Labs 02–06.
    • Day 4 · Speed and structure (~2 h). The four B dossiers; walkthroughs of an ETF market maker and an options market maker. Lab 07.
    • Day 5 · Stat arb (~2 h 15 min). The six C dossiers. Labs 08–10.
    • Day 6 · Signals and forecasts (~1 h 55 min). D1–D4, F1–F3, F5 alpha combination. Labs 11–12.
    • Day 7 · Validation and live trading (~1 h 55 min). V1–V4, F4 LLMs, F6 regimes, L1–L3. Lab 13.
    • Day 8 · Mid-frequency alpha (~1 h 55 min). The seven D dossiers. Lab 20.
    • Day 9 · Portfolio construction (~2 h 15 min). P1–P7, then the stat-arb walkthrough. Labs 14–15.
    • Day 10 · Execution (~2 h 10 min). E1–E6, then the $500M order walkthrough. Labs 16–18.
    • Day 11 · Factors, volatility, futures (~2 h 10 min). The E, F and G dossiers. Lab 19.
    • Day 12 · Cross-cutting (~2 h 30 min). The five H pages, led by the failure gallery; then quizzes 02 and 03.
    • Day 13 · Plumbing and rules (~2 h 30 min). The rest of module 01, 05 infrastructure and 06 risk and regulation.
    • Day 14 · People and judgment (~2 h 45 min). 04 industry and firms (skim the profiles), 07 conversation playbook and 08 calibration; the remaining quizzes and a first pass through the flashcards (self-test).
